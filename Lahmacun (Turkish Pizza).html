Lahmacun (Turkish Pizza)

Why our recipe?

Lahmacun is a traditional Turkish dish known for its flavorful and aromatic topping.With simple ingredients and straightforward steps, this recipe is perfect for a quick and delicious meal.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb (450g) ground lamb or beef
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 large tomato, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 pieces of flatbread or pizza dough
  • Lemon wedges (for serving)

Watch the Recipe Video:

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine the ground lamb or beef, chopped onion, minced garlic, chopped tomato, parsley, tomato paste, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, allspice, salt, and pepper. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly incorporated.
  3. Roll out the flatbread or pizza dough into thin rounds.
  4. Spread the meat mixture evenly over each piece of flatbread or dough, pressing it down gently.
  5. Place the lahmacun on a baking sheet and bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the edges are crispy and the meat is cooked through.
  6. Serve hot, with lemon wedges on the side for squeezing over the top.
