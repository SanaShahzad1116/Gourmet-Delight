Afgani Kabuli Pulao

Why our recipe?

Kabuli Pulao is known for its aromatic spices and tender lamb, creating a rich and flavorful dish. This dish is a beloved traditional recipe in Afghanistan, often enjoyed during special occasions and celebrations./p>

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs (900g) lamb, cubed
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 carrots, julienned
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/4 cup slivered almonds
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil or ghee
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground cloves
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Watch the Recipe Video:

Instructions:

  1. Heat the oil or ghee in a large pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
  2. Add the cubed lamb to the pot and brown on all sides.
  3. Stir in the ground cumin, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and salt. Cook for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add enough water to cover the lamb. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 1 hour, or until the lamb is tender.
  5. While the lamb is cooking, rinse the basmati rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Soak the rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
  6. In a separate pan, heat a little oil or ghee and sauté the julienned carrots until they are tender. Add the raisins and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the raisins are plump. Set aside.
  7. Once the lamb is tender, remove it from the pot and set aside. Reserve the cooking liquid.
  8. In the same pot, add the soaked and drained rice. Pour in the reserved cooking liquid, adding more water if needed to cover the rice by about 1 inch. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for about 15-20 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
  9. Gently stir the cooked lamb back into the rice. Top with the sautéed carrots, raisins, and slivered almonds.
  10. Cover the pot and let it sit for 5-10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together.
  11. Serve the Kabuli Pulao hot, garnished with additional slivered almonds if desired.
