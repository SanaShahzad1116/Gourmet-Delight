Haleem

Why our recipe?

Haleem is known for its rich, hearty flavor, achieved by slow-cooking a blend of grains, lentils, and meat with aromatic spices. This dish is packed with protein from the meat and lentils, as well as fiber from the grains, making it a nutritious and filling meal.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup wheat grains
  • 1/2 cup barley
  • 1/2 cup split chickpeas (chana dal)
  • 1/2 cup red lentils (masoor dal)
  • 1/2 cup yellow lentils (moong dal)
  • 1 lb (450g) boneless beef or mutton, cut into small pieces
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup ghee or vegetable oil
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • Fried onions (for garnish)
  • Lemon wedges (for serving)

Watch the Recipe Video:

Instructions:

  1. Soak the wheat grains, barley, and lentils in water for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. In a large pot, heat the ghee or vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the sliced onions and cook until golden brown. Remove half of the fried onions and set aside for garnish.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste to the pot and sauté for a few minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add the beef or mutton pieces and cook until browned on all sides.
  5. Drain the soaked grains and lentils, and add them to the pot. Stir well to combine.
  6. Add the turmeric powder, red chili powder, ground cumin, ground coriander, garam masala, and salt. Mix well.
  7. Add enough water to cover the mixture. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 3-4 hours, stirring occasionally, until the meat is tender and the grains and lentils have broken down to form a thick, porridge-like consistency.
  8. Use a hand blender to blend the mixture until smooth, if desired.
  9. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro, fried onions, and lemon wedges.
