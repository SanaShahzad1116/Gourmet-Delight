Pie Baked Apples

Why our recipe?

This pie baked apple recipe creates the most delicious, moist loaf with loads of banana flavor. Our most popular banana bread is moist, delicious, and absolutely packed with banana flavor. Why compromise the banana flavor? Friends and family love my recipe and say it's by far the best! It tastes wonderful toasted. Enjoy!

Ingredients:

  • 4 apples such as Pippin, Mutsu, or Braeburn, plus 1 more if needed
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 3 tablespoons dark brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 store-bought refrigerator pie crust
  • 1 egg, beaten

Watch the Recipe Video:

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.
  2. Cut the top 1/2 inch from the 4 apples. Using a melon baller or spoon, dig out the insides of the apples and reserve, leaving 1/4 inch flesh next to the skin. Remove the cores and seeds and discard.
  3. Coarsely chop the scooped fruit and add it to a medium bowl; you should have at least 1 1/2 cups. If not, peel, core, and dice the additional apple and add it to the bowl. Sprinkle the lemon juice over top and toss to coat. Add the brown sugar, granulated sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt to the bowl and mix. Divide the filling among the hollowed-out apples.
  4. Roll out the pie crust and using a pastry wheel, cut it into 1/4-inch-thick strips. Brush a light coating of egg wash along the top edge of each apple. Weave the strips into a lattice atop each apple, using the egg wash to adhere. Lightly brush the top of each lattice with the egg wash.
  5. Place the apples in an 8-by-8-inch baking dish. Add 1 inch of water to the bottom of the dish. Cover with foil and bake for 20 minutes. Remove the foil and bake until the crusts are golden brown and the filling is bubbling, an additional 25 minutes. Place the apples on a cooli... to cool to room temperature before serving.
  6. You can cut a circle 1 inch larger in diameter than the top of each apple and use a small cutter to cut shapes for venting, or simply cut a few slits. Then brush the edges of the apples with the egg wash, and gently press the dough edges with the back of a fork to adhere.
