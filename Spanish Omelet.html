Spanish Omelet

Why our recipe?

The combination of refried beans, salsa, and cheese creates a delicious and hearty omelet. With simple ingredients and straightforward steps, this recipe is perfect for a quick and tasty breakfast.

Ingredients:

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup refried beans
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Monterey Jack)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Watch the Recipe Video:

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until well combined.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat until hot.
  3. Pour the eggs into the skillet and let them cook undisturbed for a minute or until the edges start to set.
  4. Spread the refried beans evenly over one half of the omelet.
  5. Top with salsa and shredded cheese.
  6. Using a spatula, carefully fold the other half of the omelet over the filling.
  7. Cook for another 1-2 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and the eggs are fully cooked.
  8. Slide the omelet onto a plate and serve warm.
